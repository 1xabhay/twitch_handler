# Focus Timer — research

Date: 2026-10-10. Goal: a research-backed structure for up-to-8-hour co-working streams.

## Bottom line

No study tests a full 8-hour schedule. What evidence supports: **fixed, predictable breaks** (self-regulators overrun), **movement/relaxation breaks** over screen breaks, a **free-choice lunch**, **lighter work after lunch**. 25/5, 52/17, 90 min are conventions, not findings. We default to **50/10 on the hour** because it's predictable for viewers who join mid-stream.

## Evidence

| Method | Finding | Source | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pomodoro vs self-regulated | Self-regulators took longer sessions/breaks → more fatigue, less concentration; same task completion | Biwer et al. 2023, doi:10.1111/bjep.12593 | Moderate |
| Pomodoro vs Flowtime vs self-reg | No productivity difference; Pomodoro fatigue rose faster | Smits et al. 2025, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12292963/ | Moderate-low |
| 52/17 | DeskTime top users (2014); later data 112/26 | https://desktime.com/blog/52-17 | Very weak (vendor) |
| 90-min ultradian | Some waking ~90-min fluctuations; no proven daytime focus clock | Lavie & Kripke 1977 (Nature) | Weak |
| Deliberate practice | Elite practice ~1–1.5 h sessions, ~4 h/day ceiling; replication weak | Ericsson 1993, doi:10.1037/0033-295X.100.3.363; Macnamara 2019 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6731745 | Weak-moderate |
| Micro-breaks meta-analysis | Vigor ↑ d=.36, fatigue ↓ d=.35; longer breaks help more | Albulescu 2022, doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0272460 | Moderate |
| Break activities | Relaxation/social helped; cognitive (email, reading) backfired | Kim, Park & Niu 2017 (J Organ Behav) | Moderate |
| Hourly 5-min walks | Better mood, less end-of-day fatigue | Bergouignan 2016, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/5094084 | Moderate |
| Green view | 40 s green view improved attention | Lee et al. 2015 | Low-moderate |
| Lunch walk/relax | Less end-of-day fatigue | Sianoja 2018, doi:10.1037/ocp0000083 | Moderate |
| Lunch autonomy | Benefit depends on choosing your activity | Trougakos 2014, doi:10.5465/amj.2011.1072 | Moderate |
| 20-20-20 | Mild/conflicting | Talens-Estarelles 2023; Johnson & Rosenfield 2023 | Weak |
| Sit/stand | Build to 2–4 h/day standing/light activity | Buckley 2015, doi:10.1136/bjsports-2015-094618 | Consensus |
| Post-lunch dip | Real circadian dip; heavy carbs worsen it | Monk 2005, doi:10.1016/j.csm.2004.12.002 | Moderate |
| Implementation intentions | "When X, I'll do Y" plans, d≈.65 | Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006, doi:10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1 | Strong (meta-analysis) |

## Default "shift" preset (offsets from start)

Kickoff 10 → Focus 50 → Break 10 → Focus 50 → Break 10 → Focus 50 → Break 10 → Focus 50 → **Lunch 60** → Re-plan 10 → Focus 50 (lighter work) → Break 10 → Focus 50 → Break 10 → Focus 40 → Recap 10. ≈5.7 h focus.

## Other presets

- **pomodoro** — 25/5, long break 15 after every 4th. Low-energy days, ≤ half a day.
- **deep** — 90/20. Deep coding days; show next switch prominently.

## On screen / chat

- Always: phase, countdown, next phase, block n/N.
- Break prompts rotate, strongest evidence first: move → relax/breathe → look far/green → water → say hi in chat. Discourage feeds/email.
- Kickoff: post goals + first step (`!task`). Recap: wins, carry-overs.
- Lunch: suggest, don't prescribe (autonomy).
